Video Factory

The publishing tool behind the YouTube channel غريبة بس حقيقية (@GharibaBasHa2e2ya).

What it does

Video Factory is a private, single-owner tool. Every day it produces a short educational video that presents one surprising but true fact, and uploads that video to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

  • It only uploads to the channel of the account that authorized it.
  • It does not read, collect or store data about any other YouTube user.
  • It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-ups.
