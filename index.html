Video Factory

A short-video publishing service by Baggio Smarts. It powers غريبة بس حقيقية (@GharibaBasHa2e2ya) and the channels of our clients.

What it does

Video Factory produces short educational videos on a schedule and uploads them, through the YouTube Data API, to YouTube channels whose owners have signed up for the service and authorized it.

  • It only uploads to a channel whose owner explicitly authorized it.
  • It never reads, collects or stores data about viewers or other YouTube users.
  • Clients can stop the service and revoke access at any time.
