Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy describes how Video Factory ("the app"), a service operated by Baggio Smarts, handles information. The app publishes videos to YouTube channels whose owners have authorized it, including @GharibaBasHa2e2ya and the channels of our clients.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

The app requests a single permission, youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos and set their title, description, tags and privacy status on the authorizing account's own channel. It does not read comments, subscribers, watch history, or any other account data, and it does not access data about other YouTube users.

How information is stored

For each authorized channel we keep one OAuth refresh token, stored as an encrypted secret in a private automation environment operated by Baggio Smarts. It is used only to upload that channel's scheduled videos and is never shared, sold or transferred to anyone. We also keep the channel name and the videos' titles and schedule to show the client their dashboard.

Retention and deletion

When a client ends the service or asks us to, we delete their token and schedule data within 7 days. Clients can request deletion at the contact address below.

Cookies and tracking

The app and this website do not use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings. Once revoked, the stored token stops working immediately, and we delete it.
